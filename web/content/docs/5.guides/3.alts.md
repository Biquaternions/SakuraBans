When players join, SakuraBans records their name and address (`alts.register-on-connection`),
except on the servers in `alts.servers-without-ip-registration` (login or limbo servers behind
a proxy). Records count for `alts.record-expiration` (default: forever).

Two accounts are **alts** when they joined from the same address.

## Address strictness

`enforcement.address-strictness` decides how far an address punishment reaches:

| Level | An address ban applies to |
|---|---|
| `LENIENT` | That address only. |
| `NORMAL` | Accounts that joined from it. |
| `STERN` | One more hop through shared addresses beyond `NORMAL`. |
| `STRICT` | Player bans also cover all of the player's addresses. |

`/ban` bans a player; `/ipban` (or `--ip`) bans the player together with their current address.
`enforcement.composite-by-default: true` makes `/ban` include the address unless told
otherwise. The [allow list](/docs/commands/administration#allow-list) names accounts and
addresses that address bans don't reach.

## Finding alts

- `/alts <target> [strictness]` lists accounts sharing an address, at the configured or the
  given strictness.
- `/iphistory`, `/namehistory`, `/lastuuid` and `/ipreport` show the records.
- `/sb unlink <target> [address]` forgets an account's addresses.

Staff with `sakurabans.alts.notify` are told when a player with alts joins
(`alts.join-notification`: only when an alt is punished by default, from 2 alts, at most 20
listed, at most once per player per 30 minutes).

On a network, keep `address-strictness`, `servers-without-ip-registration` and
`record-expiration` the same everywhere, or a player one backend lets in may be turned away by
another. Overrides in the database (the [web editor](/docs/web-editor)) do this for you.
