When players join, SakuraBans records their name and address (`alts.register-on-connection`),
except on the servers in `alts.servers-without-ip-registration` (login or limbo servers behind
a proxy). Records count for `alts.record-expiration` (default: forever).

Two accounts are **alts** when they joined from the same address.

## Address strictness

`enforcement.address-strictness` decides how far an address punishment reaches:

| Level | An address ban applies to |
|---|---|
| `LENIENT` | That address only. |
| `NORMAL` | Accounts that joined from it. |
| `STERN` | One more hop through shared addresses beyond `NORMAL`. |
| `STRICT` | Player bans also cover all of the player's addresses. |

`/ban` bans a player; `/ipban` (or `--ip`) bans the player together with their current address.
`enforcement.composite-by-default: true` makes `/ban` include the address unless told
otherwise. The [allow list](/docs/commands/administration#allow-list) names accounts and
addresses that address bans don't reach.

## Finding alts

- `/alts <target> [strictness]` lists accounts sharing an address, at the configured or the
  given strictness.
- `/iphistory`, `/namehistory`, `/lastuuid` and `/ipreport` show the records.
- `/sb unlink <target> [address]` forgets an account's addresses.

Staff with `sakurabans.alts.notify` are told when a player with alts joins
(`alts.join-notification`: only when an alt is punished by default, from 2 alts, at most 20
listed, at most once per player per 30 minutes).

## Automatic alt bans

With `alts.auto-ban: ON_BAN`, banning a player bans their linked accounts too: the accounts
`/alts` would list, minus allow-listed, hidden, exempt and already banned ones. The copies
keep the first ban's reason, scope, expiry and operator, and their source is `auto`. Staff get
one notice naming them, on every server. `ON_JOIN` also bans an account the moment an address
or composite ban turns it away, so the ban follows the account to a new address. A `/ban`
that would copy itself to more than `alts.auto-ban-confirm-above` accounts asks for
`--confirm`.

This bans accounts nobody looked at, and one shared address (a school, a café, a mobile
carrier) can link strangers. That is why nothing happens until the console runs
`/sb accept alts` once (stored in the database, so it holds on every server). Revoking the
first ban leaves the copies in place: lift them one by one, or with `/staffrollback` for that
operator. Unlike LiteBans there is no third, "extreme" level.

## IP-ban decay

A ban of a player *and* their address keeps every account on that address out. After
`enforcement.ip-ban-decay` (for example `30d`) the address part stops counting: the ban keeps
the player out, wherever they join from, but no longer anyone else on that address. Nothing
is rewritten; the age of the ban decides each time, so shortening, lengthening or removing the
setting takes effect at once. Plain address bans, ranges and mutes are never affected.

`alts.hidden-players` keeps accounts and addresses out of these listings: staff accounts, or
a school or office address many players share. They are still recorded, and address bans
still reach through them; only `/alts`, `/ipreport` and the join notification leave them out,
for staff without `sakurabans.alts.viewhidden`.

On a network, keep `address-strictness`, `servers-without-ip-registration` and
`record-expiration` the same everywhere, or a player one backend lets in may be turned away by
another. Overrides in the database (the [web editor](/docs/web-editor)) do this for you.
