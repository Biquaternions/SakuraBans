title: Looking up
description: Active punishments, history and lists.
navigation:
  icon: i-lucide-search
---

| Command | Arguments | Permission | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/checkban` | `<target>` | `sakurabans.ban.check` | Active bans of the target. |
| `/checkmute` | `<target>` | `sakurabans.mute.check` | Active mutes. |
| `/checkwarn` | `<target>` | `sakurabans.warn.check` | Active warnings. |
| `/history` | `<target> [page]` | `sakurabans.list.history` | A player's punishment history. |
| `/staffhistory` | `<staff> [page]` | `sakurabans.list.staffhistory` | The punishments a staff member issued. |
| `/warns` | `[target] [page]` | `sakurabans.list.warns`, or `sakurabans.list.warns.self` for your own | Active warnings. Without a target, your own. |
| `/banlist` | `[page]` | `sakurabans.list.banlist` | Active bans. |
| `/mutelist` | `[page]` | `sakurabans.list.mutelist` | Active mutes. |
| `/warnlist` | `[page]` | `sakurabans.list.warnlist` | Active warnings of everyone. |
| `/sb info` | `<id>` | `sakurabans.info` | One punishment in detail; with `sakurabans.admin.audit` also its audit trail. |

Ids are shown as `display.id-style` says (numbers, scrambled ids or both); commands accept
either form. Addresses are censored for staff without `sakurabans.viewips` when
`display.censor-ip-addresses` is on.
