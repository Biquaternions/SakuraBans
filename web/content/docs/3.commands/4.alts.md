---
title: Alts
description: Accounts that share addresses.
navigation:
  icon: i-lucide-users
---

| Command | Arguments | Permission | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/alts` | `<target> [strictness]` | `sakurabans.alts.check` | Accounts that share an address with the target, at the configured address strictness or the one given (`LENIENT`, `NORMAL`, `STERN`, `STRICT`). |
| `/iphistory` | `<target>` | `sakurabans.alts.iphistory` | Addresses a player joined from. |
| `/namehistory` | `<target>` | `sakurabans.alts.namehistory` | Names a player used. |
| `/lastuuid` | `<name>` | `sakurabans.alts.lastuuid` | The last account that used a name. |
| `/ipreport` | — | `sakurabans.alts.ipreport` | Online players who share an address. |
| `/sb unlink` | `<target> [address]` | `sakurabans.alts.unlink` | Forget the addresses of an account (or one of them). |

Staff with `sakurabans.alts.notify` are told about joining players' alts; see
`alts.join-notification` in [config.yml](/docs/configuration/config#alts) and
[Alts and address strictness](/docs/guides/alts).
