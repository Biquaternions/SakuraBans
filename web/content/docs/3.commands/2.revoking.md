```
/unban <target> [flags…] [-r <reason…>]
```

| Command | Permission |
|---|---|
| `/unban`, `/pardon` (unban under vanilla's name) | `sakurabans.ban.revoke`, or `sakurabans.ban.revoke.own` for your own |
| `/unmute` | `sakurabans.mute.revoke` or `sakurabans.mute.revoke.own` |
| `/unwarn` | `sakurabans.warn.revoke` or `sakurabans.warn.revoke.own` |

The target may be a player, an address, a range or a punishment id (`#42`).

| Flag | Permission | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `-r`, `--reason <text…>` | — | The revocation reason (required when `reasons.require.revoke` is on). |
| `-s`, `--silent` | `sakurabans.<type>.silent` | Only staff with the silent notice permission are told. |
| `--hide` | `sakurabans.<type>.hide` | No staff notice. |
| `--scope <scope\|all>` | scope permission | Revoke only in that scope; without it, or with `all`, every scope. |
| `--void` | `sakurabans.<type>.void` | Void it instead: the punishment ends and stops counting towards template ladders. |

Staff are told about revocations with `sakurabans.<type>.notify.revoke`. With
`exemption.protects-edits`, lower staff can't revoke punishments issued by exempt staff.
